Parent Responsibility & Items Not Permitted

Parent Responsibility

Parents and guardians must check every backpack, diaper bag, lunch bag, school bag and other container before each drop-off. Only items required for the child's care should be packed. A child must not be made responsible for carrying, declaring, guarding or accounting for an adult's cash, medication, documents or valuables.

Items Not Permitted

  • cash, cheques, gift cards, payment cards, financial records, passports, unnecessary identification documents, jewellery, expensive electronics and other valuables;
  • weapons, ammunition, alarming replicas, unnecessary knives or sharp tools, illegal drugs, cannabis, alcohol, tobacco or vaping products;
  • matches, lighters, fireworks, chemicals, cleaning products, loose batteries, magnets, cords or other choking, ingestion, strangulation, burn or poisoning hazards; and
  • unsecured medication, vitamins, herbal products, supplements or foods that conflict with allergy or safety controls.

Medication, Care Items & Educator Checks

Medication and Necessary Care Items

Medication, emergency supplies, medical devices and other restricted care items must be disclosed and handed directly to the educator. Required authorization, original-container, storage and care-plan requirements continue to apply. Medication must not be left loose or undisclosed in a child's bag.

Educator Checks

Educators are not required to search every bag at every arrival. When reasonably necessary for safety, care or compliance, an educator or Agency representative may ask questions or conduct a brief, respectful visual check of the bag's main compartments, preferably with the parent present. A check is not an inventory, guarantee or acceptance of custody. Educators will not search a child's body or clothing, electronic device, wallet, sealed correspondence or locked container.

Valuables, Loss & Unexpected Items

Valuables Loss and Unexpected Items

Cash and valuables are not childcare supplies and must remain with the parent. Unless the Agency approves a specific written arrangement, the educator, day home and Agency do not accept custody of an undeclared valuable merely because it was placed in a child's belongings.

To the fullest extent permitted by law, the educator, day home and Agency are not responsible for loss, damage, theft, disappearance or misplacement of undeclared valuables brought contrary to this form and the related policy. Nothing in this form excludes responsibility that cannot lawfully be excluded, including proven intentional misconduct or negligence where applicable.

If an unexpected prohibited, restricted or valuable item is found after drop-off, the educator will protect the children, secure the item, notify the Agency and parent, document the matter and arrange its return or other action under Agency procedure.
