Privacy & Use of Personal Information

Privacy / FOIP Statement. TARG Family Day Home Agency collects personal information under the authority of the Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act (FOIP). Information is used only for the purpose of providing licensed childcare services, ensuring safety, and meeting Alberta Family Day Home Standards. Personal information will not be shared without consent, except as required by law.

Consent to Use Personal Information. The personal information on this form is collected exclusively for administering this program, part of TARG Family Day Home Agency. It is protected under FOIP and will only be used by the concerned educator for operational, medical, emergency and accounting purposes.

Questions about this data collection can be directed to info@targetfix.com.
