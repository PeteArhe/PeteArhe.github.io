Important Family Day Home Information

Please read this information carefully before signing.

Visitors

We understand that family and friends may occasionally visit your home. For everyone’s comfort and safety:

  • Please let both the agency and parents know if you expect long-term visitors.
  • Visitors should never be left alone with the children in your care.
  • If a visitor plans to stay longer than two weeks, we kindly request that they complete a Criminal Record Check (including a Vulnerable Sector Search) whenever possible. This helps give parents peace of mind and ensures we maintain a safe environment for all children.

Maximum Number of Children

To provide quality attention and care, Alberta regulations set clear limits:

  • An educator may care for up to six children (this number does not include your own).
  • Within this group: no more than three children under 36 months (3 years), including your own; and no more than two children under 24 months (2 years), including your own.

These limits are in place to make sure each child receives the support, supervision, and nurturing environment they deserve.

Safe Sleep Practices

Children’s safety is always our top priority, especially at rest time. For infants under 12 months:

  • Only a crib may be used for sleep.
  • Cribs must have a tight-fitting sheet only.
  • Items such as blankets, bumpers, pillows, or stuffed animals are not permitted in the crib.

Late Pick-up Policy

  • Each family’s contract specifies regular hours of care.
  • If children are picked up later than the agreed-upon time, educators may, at their discretion, charge a late fee of approximately $1 per child per minute, or another reasonable amount within the $1 range.
  • If late pickups happen often, we encourage families and educators to discuss adjusting the contract to reflect the additional hours. A Change of Services Form can be completed and emailed to the agency.
  • Please ensure attendance records are kept up to date, as this helps the agency process late fees accurately.
  • Any care provided outside the regular contracted hours is considered a private arrangement between the parent and educator.

For everyone’s well-being, educators cannot care for a child more than 18 hours in any 24-hour period without written approval from the agency.

Statutory Holidays

Family Day Homes are not required to remain open on statutory holidays. Families are encouraged to contact educators early if they hope to arrange care on a holiday. Educators may choose to offer care and, if so, an additional fee per child can be charged, payable directly to the educator. Common statutory holidays include New Year’s Day, Family Day, Good Friday, Canada Day, Labour Day, Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day.

Home Safety & Orientation Process

Once your application forms are completed and returned to the agency, we’ll schedule an initial safety check and interview at your residence. During this visit we make sure your home is safe, welcoming, and ready for the children in your care; we may suggest a few small adjustments; and we will look at the spaces and equipment you plan to use and identify any items you’ll need to add before opening your day home.

When your home is ready and our safety recommendations are complete, a second visit will be scheduled to review the agency forms and your resource binder and finalize the contracts together. The entire process usually takes two to three weeks, depending on how quickly security checks and references are returned to our office.

The Agency will provide you with a resource binder (hardcopy or softcopy) that typically includes Agency policies and procedures, Government regulations, Emergency procedures, Record-keeping forms, Health & safety checklists, Educational resources, and Contact information.

How We Help / Agency Support

  1. Provide ongoing training, support, and professional development to enhance educator skills.
  2. Ensure educators have access to curriculum resources and activity planning support.
  3. Offer guidance to meet and maintain licensing and quality standards.
  4. Provide access to backup or alternate care when required.
  5. Collect payments from families and distribute fees to educators.
  6. Maintain regular communication and feedback to support continuous improvement.
  7. Support access to subsidized childcare programs where applicable.
  8. Offer a fair and transparent dispute resolution process between educators and families.
  9. Refer families to appropriate community resources and support.
  10. Assist educators during emergency situations.

Summary of Required Application Processing Documents

  1. First Aid and CPR Certification: A valid certificate in First Aid and CPR is required.
  2. Criminal Record Checks: Must be completed and dated within six months of opening your day home and renewed every three years. All household members 18 years and older must also complete one. Children in the household aged 12 to 17 require a signed statement from the educator confirming that they have no criminal history.
  3. Proof of General Liability (or Extended Home) Insurance: A copy of your home insurance policy extended to cover family day home operations, equivalent to General Liability Insurance of up to $2,000,000.
  4. Pet Vaccination Records: If you have pets, please provide up-to-date vaccination records.
  5. Medical Statement: A signed statement from your physician confirming that you are both physically and mentally capable of caring for children.
  6. Professional References: Names and contact details of three professional references (not family members).
  7. Educator’s Program Information (if already prepared): daily routines, planned activities, learning materials, safety procedures and how your day is structured.
  8. Child Guidance Policies (if already prepared): your approach to guiding children’s behaviour (e.g. FLIGHT, Montessori, Reggio Emilia, High Scope). If not yet prepared, the agency will provide guidance and resources.
  9. ECE Certification – Level 1: If you do not already hold ECE Level 1 or higher, you must be willing to complete it within six months of approval. Alberta offers a free online program (alberta.ca/early-childhood-educator-certification). More information: alberta.ca/child-care.

If you encounter difficulties obtaining any of the required documents, please inform us so we can explore how best to assist you.
